steam and extraction are two different jobs. steam is high pressure steam that disinfects the surface of a mattress, sofa or blind. extraction is the wet shampoo machine, which draws grime out of upholstery instead. the sofa, the mattress edges and the blind here are steam, the quilted mattress is extraction. Steam Clean covers furniture, Curtain & Blind Steam Clean covers blinds, and both are at scrubbys.sg with yuu points on the booking. #scrubbys #steamclean #sgcleaning
